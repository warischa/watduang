TEXT CONTRAST · what sits on the layer, and the numbers

Method. WCAG 2.x. Relative luminance L = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B on linearised sRGB channels (c/255, then c/12.92 if c <= 0.04045 else ((c + 0.055)/1.055)^2.4). Ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), lighter over darker. The layer is blended in sRGB space, composite = punch x (1 - a) + sky x a per channel, which is what a browser does for opacity; a is the layer's opacity, and it is the PEAK alpha because both blobs are one element and the blob centres are opaque sky (a point never exceeds the element's opacity, and the blobs do not overlap). Computed by a script run in this session (Python, the formula above), not read off a screenshot. Text sitting on the layer: the h1 (white, #ffffff) and the lead line (ink, #14142b). The badge, the highlighted words, the CTA and the featured card are opaque gold chips with their own ground, so the layer cannot reach their text.

Reading. The layer's BRIGHTEST point is the unlit ground (sky only darkens punch): the white h1 is at today's 3.37, never lower, and rises to 3.76 / 3.95 under the layer (3.03 / 3.11 against 2.85 on a dot, which is today's worst case). The layer's DARKEST point sets the ink lead's floor: 4.79 at 0.12, 4.57 at 0.18, down from 5.35. That is a real drop, not "unchanged": ink and white pull opposite ways on any layer that is not isoluminant with punch, and no palette colour is. The lead is 22px / 600 at 1440 and 17px / 600 at 320; weight 600 is not WCAG bold, so it needs 4.5:1, which both strengths keep. Gold is out, by arithmetic: gold over punch at 0.10 takes the white h1 to 3.23 and at 0.25 to 2.95, under today's 3.37 (and under 3:1 at 0.25). The 5px ink hard shadow on the h1 is not counted in any figure.

NOTES · sources, palette, what is not pinned

Thai strings, every one verbatim from src: hero copy (badge, h1 lines, highlighted words, lead, CTA label) and the ad strip's "ช่องโฆษณา" = the props and markup in src/pages/index.astro; featured card name and tagline = names.th and tagline in src/games/croc-bite.ts (the featured game, as captured); the three nav labels and the brand = src/components/PageChrome.astro. Invented Thai: 0.

Palette, every colour on this sheet: #ff3d7f, #ffcc00, #2b7bff, #14142b, #fff6e0, #3d3b5c, #c9c3a8 (src/styles/tokens.css values, ADR-0069), #ffffff and transparent (named neutrals), rgba(255,255,255,0.18) (--hero-dot), rgba(20,20,43,0.28) (--art-shadow). The layer itself uses only #2b7bff (as var(--accent-sky) in the replay's token block). No new accent hex.

Not pinned here, and why: (1) the transform range, period, easing, reduced form: the motion sheet owns them. (2) A 1440 layer's GPU cost and texture memory: Phase 0 measured one box at 390 only. (3) Real-phone cost and battery: unmeasured, both Phase 0 lanes were Mac CPU (gh#141). (4) Hero heights at 320 / 390 / 1440 are estimates from the audit's ad-slot rects and the captures; re-measure at render. (5) Safari and Firefox paint of radial-gradient with a will-change layer: unmeasured. (6) This board has not been rendered by me: no browser was run, so a look claim waited for the owner's render; the owner approved the look on 2026-10-25 (popup). (7) Phase 0's shots/visual-hero-* images, which the README names as the A2 hero crops, are not on disk in this worktree, so the A1 look was never reviewed by eye; this board is the first picture of it.

Phase 3 code, from this board: one new element as the hero's first child, the .ambient block above verbatim (inset, z-index, pointer-events, opacity, the two radial-gradients, will-change, transform), isolation: isolate on .hero, and a reduced-motion rule from the motion sheet. The page's existing blanket reduced-motion rule in src/pages/index.astro already sets animation and transition to none for every element in main.

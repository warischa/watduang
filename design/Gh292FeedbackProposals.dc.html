A - two pop cards, each its own form and submit. The card head carries the accent: gold for feedback, punch for a game idea. 1440: side by side; 320: stacked, feedback first. The first card is drawn with its focus ring, the site's own keyboard ring (3px ink, 3px offset; gh#288).
B - one card, one form. A two-pill chooser (native radio buttons, punch fill when chosen). "ชื่อเกมหรือไอเดีย" shows only while "เสนอเกมใหม่" is chosen, done in CSS with :has() and no script; the message placeholder follows the choice the same way. Shorter page, one submit. Cost: the chooser is one more tap, and a browser without :has() shows the game-name field always (it stays optional).
Both: fields are 16px text so iOS does not zoom on focus; inputs 52px tall, submit 56px (both clear 44x44). Submit is full width at 320. The privacy line sits once, under the form(s). The back link reuses the tools hub's back-link card.

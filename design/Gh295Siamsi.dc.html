B5 · TODAY · 390 · what the keep button does (captured from dist/)

In renderSlip the keep button (BTN_KEEP) calls renderDone(DONE_KEEP); renderDone empties the stage with replaceChildren(), so the slip panel is thrown away and one line plus BTN_AGAIN is left. The tie button (BTN_TIE) does the same with DONE_TIE. The copy says "kept" while the screen has just removed the slip. The text-alignment half of B5 is not drawn here (design/SiamsiSlip.dc.html owns it); the frames below keep today's alignment as built.

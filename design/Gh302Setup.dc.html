NAME FIT · Setup (A, picked) · the rule, and the widest names drawn

Rule. The owner's name limit is 12 grapheme clusters (UAX #29; gh#308 counts them with Intl.Segmenter, so a letter with its marks is one cluster, as gh#304 does). A name is never clipped and never truncated. Each surface below has a size and a floor. A name renders at the size; if the name plus 2% slack does not fit its box, it shrinks in 1px steps; at the floor it wraps onto a second line, and every surface is sized to hold two lines at its floor (the name field instead scrolls, as a typed input does). Floors: each surface's smallest allowed size, a legibility minimum for its viewing distance; a name that does not fit on one line at its floor wraps. The widest names drawn: WWWWWWWWWWWW (W, the widest Latin letter) and ฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒ (U+0E12, the widest Thai letter); the 2% slack covers letters wider in one face only (U+0E13 in Sarabun 700, m in Sarabun 400, both within 1%). Marks: Thai above and below marks stack inside their cluster and add no width, but U+0E33 (SARA AM) is a spacing mark that joins its cluster and adds width: ฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำ is 12 clusters and measures 58% wider than U+0E12 x12 (Sarabun 700 at 20px: 333 vs 210px). It is drawn. Which names wrap where is in the table below. This fit depends on gh#308's join rules, enforced by the server: names allow-listed to Thai and ASCII letters and digits, space and . - ' _; at most 3 code points per grapheme cluster; at most one U+0E33 per cluster, and only after a Thai consonant or tone mark. Under those rules the 12-cluster U+0E12 U+0E33 x12 is the widest valid Thai name (U+0E13 U+0E33 x12 is 0.6% wider in Sarabun 700, inside the slack), and a run of repeated U+0E33, which UAX #29 keeps as one cluster, is refused at join, so it is not a fit case. Were the rules loosened, this table would no longer hold. The render fails if any name clips, wraps above its floor or onto a third line, loses its slack while on one line, or renders below its floor.

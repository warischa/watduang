RULES CHECKED · ui-ux-pro-max 2.13.0 (design input, gh#299 owner ruling 2026-11-11)

Queried 2026-11-11, one intent per query, rules taken and never its palette or fonts; no --persist, no --design-system. --domain ux "animation duration timing easing", "reduced motion", "excessive motion focal point", "projector legibility contrast large display distance", "color not only indicator", "flashing seizure flash" (returned nothing on flashing); --domain gsap "stagger timeline sequence", "countdown reveal timing" (scroll-reveal results only, not used); --stack threejs "particles performance instanced", "instanced mesh many objects draw calls", "webgl fallback context lost", "dispose memory pixel ratio", "camera depth lighting bloom", "text labels sprites canvas texture".

  • Excessive Motion (1 to 2 key elements per view): A animates the burst and its wave; B the burst, then the sweep. The chrome never moves. Checked on both.
  • Reduced Motion / Motion Sensitivity (present the final readable state): the RM frames are those end states; the countdown still steps (reduce, not remove).
  • Easing Functions: ease-out on arrivals (numeral, burst), linear on the count and the sweep (constant-rate progress).
  • Duration Timing (no one duration for everything): beats run 300ms to 3000ms by what each one carries; the countdown second belongs to the room.
  • Stagger List (gsap, 0.02 to 0.04s per item past 10 items): the dark wave staggers 0.02s per lantern.
  • Color Only: a name that did not pass goes dark AND dim AND struck through; the legend says it in words.
  • Color Contrast (4.5:1): every text pair below clears it; computed, not measured on a projector.
  • threejs: Particle Count Ceiling (start at 3000, profile before raising: B's one burst is drawn at 3000, A's at 1200 each); Points plus BufferGeometry for every burst and trail; InstancedMesh for the lanterns (up to 200; 50+ repeated meshes); Single Renderer Per Page; Pixel Ratio Cap at 2; dispose geometry, material and the name atlas texture when the round's scene goes; canvas role="img" with an aria-label, the round announced in a live region outside the canvas (ADR-0051).
  • Not from the skill: flashing. Its flash query returned nothing; WCAG 2.3.1 (no more than three flashes in any one second) is applied here from outside it: bursts are at least 400ms apart and no burst flashes the whole frame white.

NAME FIT · the gh#302 rule, on this board's surfaces

Rule (the gh#302 picked boards' NAME FIT row, followed here): the limit is 12 grapheme clusters. A name is never clipped and never truncated. It renders at its surface's size; if the name plus 2% slack does not fit its box, it shrinks in 1px steps; at the floor it wraps onto a second line (overflow-wrap: anywhere), and every surface holds two lines at its floor (in the name fields the row that holds a wrapped name grows, and the render fails if the field overflows its box). Each floor is the smallest size at which the widest 12-base-letter names, WWWWWWWWWWWW and ฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒ (U+0E12, the widest Thai letter), stay on one line, measured on this board's own frames. ฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำ (U+0E12 U+0E33 x12: 12 clusters, SARA AM takes width, 58% wider) is drawn and is the case that wraps. All three are drawn among the joined names, the names dealt and the finalists. The render fails on a clip, a name that wraps above its floor or onto a third line, a one-line name without its slack, a name below its floor, or a field whose rows overflow it.

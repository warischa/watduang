NOTES · decisions, the gap left for the owner, ui-ux-pro-max 2.13.0 rules used (rules only, never its palette or fonts)

THE GAP (PD-306-01), for the owner: the 16px floor holds at every count to 200 (frames 103, 150, 200, 200 result). The board's other promise does not: "every surface holds two lines at its floor". At 200 a card's name box is 87.8px wide (10 columns: the 66px rank column stays). W x12 and U+0E12 x12 take two lines, but U+0E12 U+0E33 x12 (the widest valid name, 253px at 16px) takes 3 lines, and 4 when bold on a passing card; its row grows (64px tall). At 200 the 20 rows of 32px use 716px before any name passes two lines (20 x 32 + 19 x 4), so 44px is spare: with the drawn worst case 28px is left (reveal) and 12px (result, bold). By that arithmetic two rows holding a 3-line name, or one holding a 4-line name, fit; more would push the field past 760px. No 200-card geometry holds that name on two lines at 16px with the 30px rank beside it: two lines need a name box of at least 127px (134px bold), so at most 8 columns, so 25 rows, and 25 rows of two 16px lines need 25 x 32 + 24 x 4 = 896px, past 760. Choices: (a) accept up to four lines for a name made of SARA AM clusters, rows growing into the spare height (drawn); (b) a name past two lines takes two cells (the gh#314 i4-fit C mechanism); (c) a lower floor for that name only. No floor was lowered on this canvas.

Execution choice, named: above 102 the row shortens to 32px before the card narrows, so at 103 the card keeps its full 293px width (6 columns, 18 rows) instead of narrowing to 7 columns: width is what holds a name, and the pick's goal is the 16px floor. From 150 up the card narrows (8, then 10 columns). The rule as written for code: n at most 102, the board's 6 columns of 40px rows; above, rows of 32px and the fewest columns c from 6 up with ceil(n / c) x 32 + (ceil(n / c) - 1) x 4 at most 760.
readable-font-size (16px minimum on mobile; the board's 16px card floor on the projector): measured on painted pixels in frame px, never computed styles alone; on the phone no text is under 16px. Color Contrast (3:1 for non-text graphics such as the rails, 4.5:1 for text): the rails are measured from the PNG, both pairs listed. horizontal-scroll (content fits the viewport width): each phone frame is loaded alone in a real viewport of its width and its scroll width read; a 400px box injected into the same page must make it scroll (must-red). visual-hierarchy: the winner leads the phone result; the HUD line stays clear of the rail. truncation-strategy (prefer wrapping over truncation): names shrink to their floor, then wrap, never clip.

NOTES · ui-ux-pro-max 2.13.0 rules used (rules only, never its palette or fonts)

touch-target-size (min 44 x 44): every tap target is measured; in pin A and C the target is the row, not a cell. touch-spacing (min 8px between targets): why six separate boxes cannot fit at 320 (304px needed). readable-font-size (16px minimum on mobile): no text in a frame is under 16px; names keep the board's 17px floor. horizontal-scroll (content fits the viewport width): every box is measured inside its parent and inside the frame on both axes; the control is the one that fails. scroll-behavior (avoid nested scroll regions that interfere with the main scroll): the reason for list B. truncation-strategy (prefer wrapping over truncation): names wrap at the floor, never clip.

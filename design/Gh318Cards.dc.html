RULES CHECKED · ui-ux-pro-max 2.5.0 (design input, gh#299 owner ruling 2026-11-11)

Queried 2026-11-16, one intent per query, rules taken and never its palette or fonts; no --persist, no --design-system. --domain ux "animation reveal duration timing easing", "reduced motion", "projector legibility contrast large display distance readability", "color not only indicator".

  • Duration Timing (150-300ms micro-interactions, never over 500ms for UI): the plate drop and the claim fade are 200-300ms; the 600ms object turn is a staged game beat, not a UI micro-interaction (the gh#303 picked flip was 600ms too).
  • Easing Functions (ease-out entering, ease-in-out a turn, never linear for UI): the flap lift and the card turn ease; the prize count does not roll (a final has no elimination count).
  • Excessive Motion (animate 1-2 key elements per view): A opens ONE object at a time; B one collective turn after a drumroll. The chrome (top bar, plates, band) is still.
  • Reduced Motion (respect prefers-reduced-motion; ADR-0046 reduce, not remove): the reveal runs the 2D path, the gh#302 picked result frame, reached by 200ms fades last slot to prize 1; the round state is still drawn and the round still finishes.
  • Color Only (never information by colour alone): a prize slot says "รางวัลที่ k" and a no-prize slot says "ไม่มีรางวัล" with "ขอบคุณที่ร่วมลุ้น" in words; the gold ring / dim type only reinforce it. Each plate names its finalist in words.
  • Color Contrast (4.5:1 normal text): every text pair in the VALUES contrast table is computed at its own surface, not measured on a projector. Size for distance: non-name text renders at 30px or more (the render reads computed size x the transform chain); names shrink only to their NAME FIT floor.

NAME FIT · the gh#302 rule, on this board's surfaces

Rule (the gh#302 picked boards' NAME FIT row, followed here): the limit is 12 grapheme clusters. A name is never clipped and never truncated. It renders at its surface's size; if the name plus 2% slack does not fit its box, it shrinks in 1px steps; at the floor it wraps onto a second line (overflow-wrap: anywhere), and every surface holds two lines at its floor (in the name fields the row that holds a wrapped name grows, and the render fails if the field overflows its box). Each floor is the smallest size at which the widest 12-base-letter names, WWWWWWWWWWWW and ฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒฒ (U+0E12, the widest Thai letter), stay on one line, measured on this board's own frames. ฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำฒำ (U+0E12 U+0E33 x12: 12 clusters, SARA AM takes width, 58% wider) is drawn and is the case that wraps. All three are drawn among the joined names, the names dealt and the finalists. The render fails on a clip, a name that wraps above its floor or onto a third line, a one-line name without its slack, a name below its floor, or a field whose rows overflow it.

G329-D4 · A CHAIN WORD NOBODY HOLDS · the same round at 10 heads, a half-cut 10 to 5

Holders are hashed over the chain's 5 positions (chain.ts: a phone knows neither the roster nor the group size), so at small cuts a chain word can be held by nobody. gh#323 evidence (sweep A): chains with a word nobody holds, 3,870 of 4,032 at 10 to 5; 2,527 of 5,436 at 20 to 10; about 0 from 40 to 20 up. Drawn: the same round as the 200-head fixture, at 10 heads; group 0 holds 1, 0, 1, 1, 2 of the drawn chain (the hash, read 2026-11-20), so มะละกอ, the second word, is held by nobody. It still carries the link (its last letter starts องุ่น), so the chain cannot skip it. The phone cannot tell either (it has no roster), so both options change the main screen only. Not offered: a chain that shortens with the cut, an engine change (gh#323 open risk), not a canvas option.

G329-D5 · THE LINK LETTER · 200 to 100, the whole drawn chain

The link rule (owner ruling 2026-11-19, gh#329): a Thai word joins only when its last written character is a consonant of the 44-consonant table; the next word starts with that consonant, leading vowels skipped (เกลือ to แอปเปิล links on อ, the leading แ skipped). A highlights the letter in both words; the highlight takes the whole grapheme cluster (น้ำ in น้ำส้ม, ลู in ลูกชิ้น), never a consonant split from its marks. B sets the consonant alone on a tile between the words and leaves the words untouched. Holders per word from the hash: 25, 17, 17, 15, 26 = 100. The chains are chain.ts's seeded pair for this round (food, seed 48215); the other candidate is ขนมจีน · น้ำพริก · เกลือ · แอปเปิล · ลูกชิ้น.

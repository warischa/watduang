G330-D4 · GROUP MARK · the three groups told apart, event round and mini-event

A person finds their group by name, by mark and by place; tint is the third cue, never the only one. The three tints are the gh#315 zone tints (punch, sky, lilac); punch and sky differ by only 1.16:1 in lightness (CONTRAST table), so two groups can look the same to a person who cannot tell the hues apart. A draws a circle, a square and a triangle; B draws 1, 2 and 3 in a fixed order (the spoken order of สูง กลาง ต่ำ, and noun, verb, adjective in an English round: the marks do not depend on the language). Drawn on the mini-event pick screen (three live groups) and the phone group card (the event round holding). Passed groups take the kit's gold with ink type and the dimmed groups are struck through, so the verdict never rests on colour either.

G330-D5 · THE THIRD CLASS · event round, QR mode, after the code

Read from the engine (round.ts analyseHeld and holdingFor): a half-cut deal gives each person ONE value of a PAIR of the three, so the room is 100 and 100 and the third class is empty that round (which pair is hashed from the seed; here high and low). Three non-empty groups could not all cut exactly under both polarities, so a three-way deal is not an option the engine has. A keeps the three panels and positions fixed and shows the empty one as not dealt; B draws only the two groups dealt and centres them (a pair of mid and low then sits in the middle). Names stay on the phones: the screen shows the head counts.

G330-D6 · THE DRAWN WORD · the draw strip and the verdict on the panels

The MC draws a word; the group holding its class is "the word's group", and the polarity pill says whether that group survives or is out (random, the MC can switch it until the draw). Drawn here: the word สมุด, first consonant ส, อักษรสูง, polarity survive: that group passes, the other dealt group does not, the third was not dealt (D5 A). In B the consonant sits alone on its tile, never marked inside the word, because a lone mark inside a Thai word can detach from its consonant. Under the strip the panels flip at once (the whole group lands together, beat table).

NAME FIT · the gh#302 rule, on this board's surfaces

Rule (the gh#302 picked boards' NAME FIT row, followed here): the limit is 12 grapheme clusters. A name is never clipped and never truncated. It renders at its surface's size; if the name plus 2% slack does not fit its box, it shrinks in 1px steps; at the floor it wraps onto a second line (overflow-wrap: anywhere), and every surface holds two lines at its floor (in the name fields the row that holds a wrapped name grows, and the render fails if the field overflows its box). All three are drawn among the joined names, the names dealt and the finalists.

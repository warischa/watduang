DESIGN NOTES - E · Toy Box (desktop 1440). Not page content.
STRUCTURE: CHANGES ADR-0058 - NO. Same order as the live page: hero, popular row, three whole-panel intent links (/c/party/, /c/fortune/, /tools/), how-to, FAQ. landing-claims-check unaffected. The toys inside the party panel are decoration inside its one link, not a game list.
Lack 1 (no game art): featured toy at 460px on a pedestal; popular cards lead with art; three toys burst out of the party panel's top edge.
Lack 2 (no motion): hero toy squash-bounces; 4 clay balls float; CTA pulses; panel toys float; cards rise on hover.
Lack 3 (not bold): violet-to-pink candy gradient #6c4dff/#a64dff/#ff5fa2; clay coral/gold/sky panels; Mitr 700 at 92px with a 4-layer extruded text shadow.
Lack 4 (ad landing, not portal): answered by look only - the hero is a toy on a stage rather than a pitch, and every surface is a tactile toy piece. The panel structure stays; if the owner reads lack 4 as structural, E does not fix it.
3D art vs flat UI: full reconciliation - the UI goes soft 3D too (top-lit gradients, inner highlight, solid edge drop, no ink outlines), matching the toy art's material.
Motion + cost: 0 bytes JS. ~1.3 KB CSS, 3 keyframes. Idle per frame: 1 squash + 4 balls + 3 panel toys + 1 CTA pulse = 9 elements, transform only (compositor). The extruded text-shadow and the layered clay box-shadows are static paint, never animated. Art ~29 KB per webp, 6 on the page (~175 KB), hero eager, rest lazy. Reduced-motion: all animation and the hover rise off.
Invented Thai strings: 0. Hero, CTA pair, "ยอดนิยม", how-to step, FAQ, "ช่องโฆษณา" = live home page copy; panel copy = categories hubHeading/hubBody + toolsGroup heading/body; games = module names.th / tagline; nav = site chrome.
Art: croc-bite.webp is real; pinocchio-luck, how-close-is-near, dice-loser, timebomb, short-stick are PENDING (../public/art/<id>.webp; dashed box is mockup-only). The clay balls are CSS gradients, not images.
New colour values: none of these are today's --accent-* tokens; adopting them is an owner decision.

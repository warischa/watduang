occupation, means, manner, habit, distinguishing mark. **Weight is deliberately not a field.**

repeats one in `cards.md` or in the female file, and no mark repeats. Youngest is 25, so every card
is a clear adult. No occupation, habit or mark involves alcohol, a brand, or a shop name.

**Two departures from the first ten, flagged for the owner — each is one edit to reverse:**

- **Ages run to 54** (the first ten span 26–41). A reader in `40–49` or `50 ขึ้นไป` has a meeting
  age drawn at 50 or later, so without older cards every draw hands them someone decades younger.
  Whether the draw should match a card's age to the meeting age is gh#101's to decide, not this file's.
- **Two cards here carry a nationality other than ไทย** (M10, M15; two more in the female file). The
  first ten are all ไทย.

**Re-render trigger:** these portraits are rendered before sign-off. An edit that moves gender, age,
build, occupation, manner or mark on a card invalidates that card's portrait; a wording edit does not.

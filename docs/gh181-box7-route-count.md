So `how-close-is-near` and `cursed-number` are named in the ticket's own title and were **never**
measured at 1440×900 by this instrument, while six of the measured eight are outside the title.

- **If the answer is eight** — the readings cover every route, and the ticket's title and
  § What to build both still say four, so they need restating too.
- **If the answer is four** — the readings cover only two of them, and box 7 cannot close until
  `how-close-is-near` and `cursed-number` are measured at 1440×900.

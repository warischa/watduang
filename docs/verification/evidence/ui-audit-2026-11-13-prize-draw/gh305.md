## Findings

### PD-305-01 · major · setup, game picker · 1920 (measured), 1280 and 320 (same CSS, inferred)
- Rule: `disabled-states`, `state-clarity`, `error-clarity` (a disabled control must look disabled and
  say why).
- Evidence: `game-prize-draw-1920-cards-picker-r1-not-halving.png`.
  - The schedule is 40/25/10/7/5. The recheck read all four ไพ่แจกทั้งห้อง buttons as `disabled` there
    (cards runs only on an exact half).
  - They paint exactly like the enabled พลุนับถอยหลัง button beside them: ink on white, 16.66:1 for both.
  - No line says why cards cannot be picked.
  - The page styles `.sb:disabled` (opacity 0.4, the prize stepper: 2.31:1 in
    `game-prize-draw-1280-picker.png`). `.segb` has no disabled rule.
  - The MC taps, nothing happens, and nothing explains it.
- Board: no board draws this state. Gh305GamePick draws a locked choice only for 1b B (WebGL missing),
  which was not picked.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) The locked-choice look Gh305GamePick names for 1b B: dashed outline on the cream ground, muted
    text. Add a one-line reason under the row, as `[copy: owner]`.
  - (b) The page's own `.sb:disabled` treatment (opacity 0.4) on `.segb:disabled`, plus the same reason
    line.
  - (c) Hide the cards button on rounds that are not an exact half, as the final row omits it, and keep
    the reason line.

    band's left cell. It has the caption "ตัวอย่าง ไม่ใช่งานจริง" and the buttons รอบคัดออก, รอบชิง and

    - The buttons cover the name's lower half.
  - `game-prize-ladder-play-1920-idle.png`: the same bar (1-5, เปิดผล) under the รอบชิง label.

    name's descenders mid-reveal (older build; the bar's place is unchanged in the recheck shots).
- Board: no board draws the demo page; the demo module's comment says so. Gh305GamePick "FOUND WHILE
  DRAWING" measured the same collision for the projector's MC button. Gh314 1a B moved that button to the
  play band's right end, but the demo bar was not moved.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) The demo bar goes to the play band's right end, as Gh314 1a B, with the caption beside it.
  - (b) It stays bottom-left, and the frame's lower band starts its left cell past the bar's width.

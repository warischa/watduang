# gh#307 — ทางแยกเลือกเส้น (ladder final)

Screens audited:
- projector `/game/prize-draw/play/` final at 1920 and 320: lane pick (empty, 2, 5 picks), the 3D reveal
  (33 frames at 1920, 12 at 320), the result;
- demo `/game/prize-ladder/play/` at 1920 and 320. The demo bar is PD-305-02 and PD-305-03.

Shots are in `../gh305-recheck-2026-11-13/shots/` unless the path says otherwise.

## Findings

### PD-307-01 · critical · projector, lane pick · 320
- Rule: `touch-target-size`, `touch-spacing`, `no-precision-required`, `readable-font-size`.
- Evidence: `game-prize-draw-play-320-ladder-lane-pick-empty.png`, `-2.png` and `-5.png`, and the
  recheck README's finding 2.
  - The frame is scaled to about 0.17. The lane plates, their numbers and the names on them paint at
    about 5px, so the MC cannot read which lane holds which name.
  - The finalist name buttons are real pixels (`button.pd-name`, 153x52). They sit on top of the lane
    controls for lanes 2 and 3 (`.pd-lane`, 53x44, same y). `elementFromPoint` at a lane's centre returns
    the name button.
  - A tap aimed at lane 2 or 3 therefore picks a name. With a lane already selected, that writes the name
    into the selected lane, not the one aimed at.
  - Mitigations measured: a free strip of each lane control still took taps, and tapping a filled lane
    empties it.
  - The final's lane pick is the room's fairness moment, so a wrong silent assignment is the worst outcome
    on this screen.
- After the shots: the working tree adds a `data-lanes` phone layout (`play.css` and `stage.ts`, 14:36,
  with a `lanes-phone` test). It is not in the built CSS and not in any shot. Its own comment says
  "No frame draws them at phone width".
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Ratify the working-tree layout: i5-phone B's two-column rule applied to the lanes, lane controls
    drawn as the plates at 16px. Re-shoot it at 320 before closing.
  - (b) Under 640px, hide the lane controls and show a line telling the MC to pick lanes on the big
    screen (`[copy: owner]`).
  - (c) Keep the scaled frame, but move the name row below the plates so no control overlaps another.
    The plates stay about 5px.

### PD-307-04 · minor · projector, ladder rails before the reveal · 1920
- Rule: `color-contrast` for graphics (WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for graphics needed to understand the
  content).
- Evidence: `game-prize-draw-play-1920-ladder-lane-pick-empty.png`.
  - The rails paint `#5e5c7b` on the ground `#1c1b3e`: **2.58:1**.
  - Formula check: `#d9d6f0` at 35% over that ground is `#5e5c7c`, 2.59:1.
  - The travelled path is gold and fully legible (`...-ladder-reveal-06-cut-0.png`). The rails are what
    shows the room "the paths are fixed before anyone moves".
- Board: Gh303Ladder VALUES: "rails at #d9d6f0 35%". The build matches the board exactly.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Keep 35%: the rails are context, and the path carries the meaning.
  - (b) 50% alpha, which is `#7a7897`, 3.89:1 (arithmetic, not rendered).
  - (c) The dim text colour `#9a97c0` solid, 5.93:1 (arithmetic).

## Observations, not findings

- `game-prize-draw-play-1920-ladder-reveal-33-prize-1.png` is a single frame of the crossfade into the
  result: the result overlay at low alpha over the 3D plates. `fade-crossfade` is the rule's own
  recommendation, and the settled `...-ladder-result.png` is clean.
- In `...-ladder-lane-pick-2.png`, the remaining name buttons paint dimmed (text 5.55:1, against white
  15.04:1 in `-empty.png`). The `.pd-name:disabled` opacity of 0.55 matches the arm window after a repaint
  (ADR-0057), so this is transient by design. It was not read from the DOM.

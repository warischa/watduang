### PD-315-06 · minor · the stagger squeeze is not on the board · the wave is visible, the spacing is not
- Rule: `duration-timing`, `stagger-sequence`, `motion-consistency`.
- Evidence: `prize-zones-1920-mid.png`, `prize-zones-1440-mid.png`, `projector-zones-1920-mid.png` show the wave
  mid-landing (zone 1 resolved, zone 2 part-resolved, zones 3-4 "ยืนขึ้น"); a single still cannot read a per-name
  spacing, so the value is read from the plan, executed this turn: a 50-name zone gets
  `min(20, (1100 − 400) / 49)` = **14.2857 ms** a name, not the board's 20 ms. At the board's 20 ms the zone's wave
  would end at 49 × 20 + 400 = **1380 ms**, inside a 1100 ms slot — zone 2 would start while zone 1 still lands.
  The squeeze ends it at exactly 1100 ms. At the projector's 10-name zones the squeeze does not bite (20 ms holds).
- Board: MOTION row "zone wave 1..Z": "0.02s stagger per name", Z × 1100 ms. The two board values are
  incompatible above 36 names a zone; the build resolves the conflict by squeezing, silently.
- Disposition: **owner-pick** — (a) accept the squeeze (it is what keeps the zones sequential), (b) lengthen the
  slot at big zones so the board's 20 ms holds (the round grows), (c) state the squeeze on the board's MOTION row.

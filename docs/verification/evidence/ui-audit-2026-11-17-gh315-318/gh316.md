## Findings

### PD-316-01 · critical · the race itself never paints · 1440 and 1920, both routes
- Rule: `motion-meaning`, `visual-hierarchy`.
- Evidence: `prize-marbles-1920-mid.png`, `prize-marbles-1920-freeze.png`, `prize-marbles-1440-mid.png`,
  `prize-marbles-1440-freeze.png`, `projector-marbles-1920-mid.png`, `projector-marbles-1920-freeze.png`.
  - No marble, glow disc or streak appears in any 3D frame. The track shows only the name plates, the lane
    separators, the finish line and the band. At the freeze the passed plates are gold and the rest dim and
    struck, but nothing sits at the line and nothing sits stopped short of it.
  - Pixel scan of all 20 shots: 0 pixels in the pink (`#ff3d7f`) or blue (`#2b7bff`) marble-body families,
    bright or pre-composited dim, at every width and on both routes. Positive control: the same scan finds
    the gold finish line, plates and figure in every shot (18 018 gold px in `prize-marbles-1920-mid.png`).
  - The band where the marbles must be at the mid timestamp (frame x 560-950, all 20 lanes, mapped through
    the measured 0.945 frame scale) holds only track ground `#14142b` and separator `#45455c`: 0 near-white
    pixels, so no spec highlight either.
  - Must-red for the scan: a lit pink disc injected into a copy of that crop is counted (441 px); the real
    crop counts 0. The detector is not blind.
  - `console.json` for `marbles-1440`, `marbles-1920` and the projector leg: no exceptions, no console
    errors, 1 GL context created, so this is not a shader compile failure or a lost context.
  - Mechanism (inferred, not proven here): the marble, glow and streak materials are opaque while the
    full-frame background `Panel` is transparent; three renders the opaque list before the transparent
    list regardless of `renderOrder`, so the background quad overpaints the race. The plates, band, figure
    and pulse are transparent panels and do survive, which matches exactly what the shots show.
  - Why critical under this audit's severity rule: the picked M2-A moment — time stops the instant the last
    survivor crosses — is the task's essential spectacle, and at the projector widths the ticket serves it
    is not visible at all. The count climbs and the plates change colour with no visible cause; the room
    watches a scoreboard, not a race. The survivor set itself stays readable on the plates, which is the
    only reason this is not also a fairness defect.
- Board: mb-reveal A and mb-result A draw 20 marbles a heat, across-marbles gold with a glow at the line,
  stopped marbles dim short of it. None of that is on screen.
- Disposition: **fix-here**. Make the race materials share the background's transparency (or move the
  background into the opaque list) so `LAYER` order holds, then re-shoot mid and freeze at 1440 and 1920 on
  both routes and re-run the hue scan before closing.

### PD-316-09 · major · trailing heats with no survivor never run · not exhibited in any shot
- Rule: `multi-step-progress` (the heat counter is the round's step indicator), `motion-meaning`.
- Evidence: `prize-marbles-1920-mid.png` shows the counter format "ชุดที่ 6 จาก 10" — the denominator is
  always the full heat count. `prize-marbles-1920-freeze.png` (plates 181-200) and
  `projector-marbles-1920-freeze.png` (2 heats, "ชุดที่ 2 จาก 2" in its mid) show that in BOTH captured runs
  the last heat holds a survivor, so no shot exhibits a skipped trailing heat. The behaviour is read from
  the plan (`Heat.runs`, freeze heat = last heat holding a survivor): a round whose last survivor crosses
  in heat 9 ends at "ชุดที่ 9 จาก 10" and heat 10 never loads.
- Board / ticket: the pick note and its correction say "a round under M1 A plays all 10 heats". The build
  plays only up to the freeze heat. Outcomes are unchanged (the across-set still equals the record), but
  the room sees a round end one or more heats early against the stated structure, and the 77.4 s ruling
  was computed for a freeze in heat 10.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Accept the skip and correct the ticket's "all 10 heats" note; the counter then tells the truth
    about a shorter round.
  - (b) Run the trailing heats as empty lanes so the counter always reaches 10 of 10 (longer round).
  - (c) Keep the skip but change the denominator to the heats that run, so the counter never promises a
    heat that will not load (`[copy: owner]`).

## Observations, not findings

- The phone result hides `.pd-legend` and `.pd-rt` in the phone field block, so "ผ่าน / ไม่ผ่าน" and
  "รอบนี้คัดเหลือ … คน" exist only on the projector (`prize-marbles-320-result-top.png`,

  `content-priority` asks. The figure and "ผ่านรอบที่ 1" still state the count.
- The "freeze" shots are settle frames (result band up, canvas still live), not the freeze instant. The
  capture polls for the last 3D frame, and the canvas outlives the hold by the 3000ms settle. Consequence:
  the pulse and the exact freeze instant are uncovered, not passed.
- At 1440 the whole 1920 frame scales 0.75: plate names 21px font (digits 15px), idle chips 15px font
  (digits 11px). Above the phone breakpoint this is the shared frame fit, the same class phase 1 accepted;
  below 640px the 2D phone path replaces it, so no scaled 3D exists at phone width on this game.
- Reduced motion: `prize-marbles-1440-rm-idle.png` and `prize-marbles-1440-rm-result.png` are the plain 2D field and result,
  no GL context created (`console.json`), result at 165 ms. `reduced-motion` passes.
- Vertical scrolling of the demo phone pages and the demo top bar are owner-ruled (2026-11-17, PD-305-02 /
  PD-305-03 lineage); the 320/390 result shots scrolled to the go button are that ruling, not a finding.

contrast against it is the text, WCAG 2.x luminance formula.

  `#ffcc00` **11.93 both ways** (`prize-marbles-320-result-top.png`), white on `#23214d` **15.04 both ways**

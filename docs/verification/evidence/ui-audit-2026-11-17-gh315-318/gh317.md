# gh#317 — ซองของขวัญ (gift-envelope final), the ui-ux-pro-max rule set on the built screens

Step 1 (audit) only: every row is a finding plus a disposition, never a choice; nothing was fixed, no source
edit, no build, no browser run. Order after the owner picks: canvas first, then code (ADR-0033).

Screens audited (18 shots, all opened for this audit, in `shots/`):
- demo `/game/prize-envelopes/play/` at 320, 390, 1440, 1920: idle, mid, result, the 320/390 result-top
  companions, and the 1440 reduced-motion idle/result pair;
- projector `/game/prize-draw/play/` with the envelopes final at 1920: pick screen empty, pick screen with
  five picks placed, mid-reveal, result.

Picks read as text, never rendered: `design/Gh317Envelopes.dc.html` (D1 A ROW, D2 A ONE AT A TIME, E1 copy
approved — owner pick recorded on gh#317, 2026-11-16), its VALUES / NAME FIT / MOTION / COPY SOURCES / NOTES
rows, and `src/styles/tokens.css`. The 2026-11-17 owner ruling on gh#317 (each opened object holds about 7 s:
600 ms turn + 6400 ms hold, "1.5 s + 5 x 7 s + 0.2 s = 36.7 s") supersedes the board's 7 s for all five.
Rules: the skill's quick reference (plugin 2.13.0), priorities 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 — rules only, never its palette
or fonts.

Contrast: painted pixels, the method of `../ui-audit-2026-11-13-prize-draw/README.md` (crop box → most common
pixel is the ground, the 99.5th-percentile pixel by contrast against it is the text, WCAG 2.x luminance).
- Positive control: the pixel reads equal the formula on the token pairs they paint. Ink `#14142b` on gold
  `#ffcc00` is **11.93 both ways** (`shots/projector-envelopes-1920-pick-done.png`, เปิดผล); white on `#23214d`
  is **15.04 both ways** (same shot, a name plate); `#d9d6f0` on `#23214d` is **10.62 both ways**
  (`shots/projector-envelopes-1920-mid.png`, the no-prize thank-you line). `#9a97c0` on `#23214d` is 5.42 both
  ways (same shot, the no-prize label).
- Must-red: the disabled เปิดผล in `shots/projector-envelopes-1920-pick-empty.png` reads **4.31:1** (painted
  ground `#957913`, gold at reduced opacity over the night, ink text). The detector can fail a pair. Disabled
  controls are exempt from 1.4.3, so this is the control, not a finding.

Severity (my reading of the rule, not a pick): **critical** a tap lands on something other than what it aimed
at, or essential information is unreadable at a width the ticket serves; **major** the rule fails and the task
degrades but a workaround exists; **minor** polish.

Timing next to the owner-ruled figure: `timings.json` gives envelopes-1920 **36754 ms** and envelopes-1440
**36760 ms** first-press-to-result, against the ruling's 36.7 s (1500 + 5×7000 + 200); the +54…60 ms is frame
overhead. Projector leg 37270 ms. At 320/390 and under reduced motion the press paints the 2D result at once
(168 ms, no canvas) — an instant paint, not a reveal duration (`not-reached.json`).

## Findings

### PD-317-01 · major · demo, result · 320 and 390
- Rule: `readable-font-size`, `content-priority`.
- Evidence: `shots/prize-envelopes-320-result.png` (and its identical companion `shots/prize-envelopes-320-result-top.png`),
  `shots/prize-envelopes-390-result.png` / `shots/prize-envelopes-390-result-top.png`.
  - The whole 1920 frame is scaled to the page width (0.167 / 0.203). The heading ผู้ได้รับรางวัล and the prize
    line paint **8px** glyphs at 320 and **10px** at 390; the runner-up cards' label and name **6px** at 320 and
    **7px** at 390. Only the winner's name (22px at 320) reads.
  - The lower half of the 320 screen is empty night ground above the real-pixel demo bar.
  - The idle beside it (`shots/prize-envelopes-320-idle.png`) is the i5-phone B column layout with 16px chips,
    so the page jumps readable → unreadable at the press and back at ดูอีกครั้ง.
- Board / pick: PD-305-05's owner pick (Gh305AuditFrames frame 2) gives the projector result a phone column
  layout (`.pd-viewport[data-result]` in the prize-draw play sheet). The demo's own sheet keeps the scaled
  frame on purpose — its phone block's comment says "The result keeps the scaled frame, at exactly the page's
  width." The 2026-11-17 ruling covers the demo bar's place (vertical scroll), not type size; no ruling covers
  the demo result's legibility.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Extend the picked phone result layout to the demo route, as the projector already has it.
  - (b) Ratify the scaled demo result and stamp it on the board as the demo's result at phone width.
  - (c) Keep it and add a "best on a big screen" line (`[copy: owner]`).

### PD-317-02 · major · reveal, a long MC prize name in a prize slot · 1920 (arithmetic; no shot carries one)
- Rule: `truncation-strategy`, `visual-hierarchy` (text over text).
- Evidence: `shots/projector-envelopes-1920-mid.png`, `shots/prize-envelopes-1440-mid.png` show the slot's
  three blocks inside the 204px body: label 30px, prize line 30px, picker 36px (floor 20px).
  - `paintSlot` wraps the prize line with `wrapLines` at 30px over the 264px slot and caps nothing: the panel
    grows downward (37.5 + 4 + n×39 + 6 + picker lines). Slot top 447, body bottom 637, plate top 660
    (`SLOT`, `PLATE`, `ENV` in the envelopes plan).
  - One prize line ends ≈577, two ≈616, three ≈655 — past the body, 5px above the plate. Three lines plus a
    two-line picker at the 20px floor reach ≈660; four lines ≈699, into the plate's box. The plate paints above
    the slot (`LAYER.plate` over `LAYER.slot`), so the spilled line is hidden behind the pill, not wrapped into
    view: the prize name's tail disappears.
- Board: NAME FIT caps every *name* surface at two lines at its floor and fails a third; it sets no rule for
  the MC's prize line, and the slot's box is "the body width less 36px" with no height.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Cap the prize line at two lines and shrink it to a floor (a new board value).
  - (b) Let the slot grow and move the plate down with it (new board geometry).
  - (c) Accept the spill and stamp it as the long-prize behaviour.

### PD-317-03 · major · projector pick screen, free-name row over the plate controls · 640–845px (no shot; arithmetic)
- Rule: `touch-spacing`, `no-precision-required`, `z-index-management`.
- Evidence: `shots/projector-envelopes-1920-pick-empty.png` shows the row clear at scale 0.948: plate control
  bottom y≈742, name pill top y≈778, a 36px gap.
  - `.pd-lane` tops at 660×scale with height max(44px, 64px×scale); `.pd-names` tops at 760×scale (`NAMES_Y`).
    They overlap when 660s + 44 > 760s, i.e. **s < 0.44**: width-limited viewports of 640–845px (768×1024 →
    s 0.40, a 4px strip; 640×900 → s 0.333, 11px). The row is later in `.pd-picks`, so its buttons win the
    strip: a tap on a plate's bottom edge places the next name instead of selecting or emptying that object.
  - Below 640px the phone layout removes the scaled frame; at 1440/1920 s ≥ 0.75, no overlap. The ledger's
    "about 0.55" is not the threshold this CSS gives: at 0.55 the gap is 11px.
  - Second mode: five long names wrap the row to two 52px lines + 16px gap; its bottom (760s + 120) passes the
    low band's top (870s) for s < 1.09 — at 0.948 with five 12-cluster names, 16px into the band's empty top.
- Board: no frame places the row (see PD-317-11); the width band is not one the ticket serves.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Place the row in the plate-to-band gap at real pixels below a scale threshold (needs a board frame).
  - (b) Let the row's buttons scale with the frame down to the tap floor.
  - (c) Accept: tablet widths are outside the served set, and the strip is at most 11px.

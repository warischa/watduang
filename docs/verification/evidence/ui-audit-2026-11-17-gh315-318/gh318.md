## Build under test, and what the shots predate

- `dist/` is one build of 2026-11-17 10:35:45; the flipcards shots run 11:32–11:53. The renderer's `index.ts`
  and `plan.ts` were edited again at 11:43, after that build: the built chunk still chooses which side of a
  turning card shows by the clock (`faceUp`), while the tree chooses by the panel's real facing toward the
  camera (`facing`, `EDGE_ON`). Every flipcards shot therefore predates that fix — **shot predates fix** on
  anything about the turn's mid-flight appearance (PD-318-05, -08, -09). The settled faces in the mid shots
  show no artifact of the old swap.
- The round-1 fixes named for the zones route (phone idle layout, name-grid capacity) touch no flipcards
  shot; nothing here is flagged for them.
- The built chunk matches the tree on every other value cited below: `FOCAL` 2000 (`2e3`), the cubic
  `easeInOut`, the back painted at scale 2, the hatch loop `k*7+6` after `rotate(-PI/4)`.

## Timing next to the owner ruling

Owner ruling 2026-11-17: each opened object is held about 7 s. `timings.json`: flipcards-1440 **36752 ms**,
flipcards-1920 **36756 ms**, projector-flipcards-1920 **37269 ms**, first press to result; flipcards-320 /
-390 / -1440-rm **167 ms** (the instant 2D paint, not a reveal). The ruled pace is 5 × (600 turn + 6400 hold) =
35000 ms; the build adds the 1500 ms idle hold and the 200 ms claim fade (PD-318-07), so 36700 ms is expected
and the demo legs land +52/+56 ms over it. The per-object 7 s itself matches the ruling.

- Rule: `continuity`, `content-priority`, `color-not-only`.
- Evidence: `projector-flipcards-1920-result.png`, `prize-flipcards-1920-result.png`,
  `prize-flipcards-1440-result.png`, against `projector-flipcards-1920-mid.png`.
  - The board's ROUND RESULT frame (card-result) is the row standing face up: white prize faces with a gold
    ring naming each finalist, night no-prize faces with the L2 copy, the claim line under the row.
  - The settled screen is instead the shared gh#302 2D result frame: heading "ผู้ได้รับรางวัล", one gold
    winner over "รางวัลที่ 1 · …", two raised cards for prizes 2 and 3. On `complete` the host tears the
    canvas down and paints that frame (`exit('settled')` → `render2D` in the shared game host), so the
    face-up row lives only for the 200 ms claim fade and is never the settled picture.
  - The two no-prize finalists (027 and 033 on the demo leg, Guest10 and Guest35 on the projector leg) are
    named on their turned cards in the mid shot and appear nowhere on the result: their outcome is delivered
    once, then dropped.
  - No white prize face is painted in any shot of this game, so the board's prize-face language (white, gold
    ring, `#c2185b` label) is unseen, not verified.
  - The claim line is absent on every result (`claimLineInDom: false` in `console.json`); it is QR-mode only
    (`not-reached.json`), so this half is a mode limit, not a defect.
- Disposition: **owner-pick**.
  - (a) Hold the face-up row as the settled result on the projector (the host keeps the scene at `total`
    instead of handing back), keeping the 2D frame for reduced motion, below 640px and the fallback.
  - (b) Ratify the shared 2D result as every final's settled frame and stamp that on the board, accepting
    that the no-prize finalists leave the screen at the result.
  - (c) Keep the 2D frame but add the no-prize finalists to it as night cards with the L2 copy.

## Contrast readings (painted pixels)

Method as the 2026-11-13 README: crop box, most common pixel = ground, 99.5th-percentile pixel by contrast
against it = text, WCAG 2.x luminance ratio.
- **Positive control:** ink `#14142b` on gold `#ffcc00` reads **11.93** on the MC button
  (`projector-flipcards-1920-pick-done.png`) against formula 11.93; white on raised `#23214d` reads **15.04**
  on a name plate (same shot, and `prize-flipcards-1920-mid.png`) against formula 15.04; dim `#9a97c0` on
  `#23214d` reads **5.42** on the turned no-prize face (`projector-flipcards-1920-mid.png`) against formula
  5.42. The detector equals the formula on the token pairs it paints.
- **Must-red:** the disabled เปิดผล button in `projector-flipcards-1920-pick-empty.png` (opacity 0.55) reads
  **4.31:1** (painted `#957913` ground, `#14142b` text) — below 4.5, so the detector can fail a pair. Disabled
  controls are exempt; the enabled button reads 11.93.

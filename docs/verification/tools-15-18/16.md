## Class note — weakest-assertion rule, ad-slot boxes (#16-10), git-history boxes (#16-13)

A box's overall verdict is the weakest of its individual assertions — a box is PROVEN only if every
assertion inside it is PROVEN. Per the task brief's known classes: ad-slot boxes are locally checkable
for placement/reservation but never for whether an ad actually renders (owner = Google/AdSense + a
production deploy that doesn't exist yet); git-history/red-green claims are never locally checkable from
`dist/` + `driver.mjs` (owner = the immutable past, covered by a sibling agent's non-vacuity check).
#16-10 has two structural sub-claims that passed outright, but its ad-render sub-claim is unprovable
locally, so the whole box is **UNPROVABLE** — the passing sub-claims are recorded inline, not discarded,
but they do not round the box up to PROVEN. #16-13's blocking clause is the history claim itself, so
that whole box is UNPROVABLE too. #16-11 is a related but distinct case: its non-provable clause
("respects reduced motion") has no owner/unblocking-event (nothing future will make it observable), so
it is recorded UNDECIDED rather than UNPROVABLE — see that section for why.
